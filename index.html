Margherita Pizza

Classic Italian pizza with tomato sauce, mozzarella, and fresh basil

Extended Preparation Time This recipe requires more than 45 minutes to prepare. Plan accordingly!
  • 1
    1 pizza dough
  • 2
    1/2 cup tomato sauce
  • 3
    1 cup mozzarella cheese
  • 4
    Fresh basil leaves
  • 5
    1 tbsp olive oil
  • 6
    1/2 tsp salt
  • 7
    1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 8
    1/4 cup Parmesan cheese
  • 1
    Preheat oven to 475°F (245°C).
  • 2
    Roll out the pizza dough on a floured surface.
  • 3
    Spread tomato sauce evenly over the dough.
  • 4
    Add mozzarella cheese and Parmesan cheese.
  • 5
    Drizzle with olive oil.
  • 6
    Bake for 12-15 minutes until crust is golden.
  • 7
    Top with fresh basil leaves.
  • 8
    Slice and serve hot.
